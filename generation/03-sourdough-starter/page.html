Sourdough starter in 7 days

Flour, water and patience. Pick a day to see what to do and what the jar should look like.

Feeding calculator

Keepg of starter, then add flour andwater (1:1:1).

It is ready when…

It doubles in 4–8 hours

Mark the level with a rubber band after feeding (the red line in the jar) and check back.

It smells like yoghurt

Tangy and slightly sweet. Nail-polish or sock smells mean it is still settling down.

A spoonful floats

Drop a little into a glass of water. If it floats, it holds enough gas to raise bread.

Something looks wrong?

Grey liquid on top means it is hungry: pour it off and feed. No activity after day 4: move it somewhere warmer. Pink, orange or fuzzy patches are mould: start again.
