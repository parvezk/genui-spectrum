## Splitting $186.40 between 4 people

**With an 18% tip, each person pays $54.99.**

Here is how that works out, and what other tip levels would cost:

| Tip | Tip amount | Total | Per person (÷ 4) |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| No tip | $0.00 | $186.40 | $46.60 |
| 15% | $27.96 | $214.36 | $53.59 |
| **18%** | **$33.55** | **$219.95** | **$54.99** |
| 20% | $37.28 | $223.68 | $55.92 |
| 25% | $46.60 | $233.00 | $58.25 |

### The 18% calculation

1. Tip: $186.40 × 0.18 = $33.55
2. Total: $186.40 + $33.55 = $219.95
3. Per person: $219.95 ÷ 4 = $54.9875, which rounds up to **$54.99**

Four payments of $54.99 come to $219.96, one cent more than the total, so the bill is fully covered.

### Easier numbers

- **Round up to $55 each.** That collects $220.00, and the tip becomes $33.60 (18.0%).
- **Go to 20% and pay $56 each.** That collects $224.00; the tip is $37.60 (20.2%).
