## Reporting a bug in the mobile app

Sorry you ran into a problem. To get this to the right team quickly, I need the following. The first five are essential; the rest speed things up.

**Essential**

1. **What went wrong, in one sentence.** For example: "The app crashes when I tap Pay."
2. **Where in the app?** Login, Checkout, Search, Notifications, Profile, or somewhere else.
3. **Steps to reproduce.** What you did, one step per line, from opening the app.
4. **What you expected** and **what happened instead.**
5. **Your device.** Phone model and OS version (for example iPhone 15, iOS 17.5 or Pixel 8, Android 14).

**Helpful**

6. **App version.** In the app: Profile → Settings → About.
7. **How often it happens.** Every time, sometimes, or just once.
8. **How bad it is.** Does it block you completely, is there a workaround, or is it cosmetic?
9. **A screenshot or screen recording**, if you have one.
10. **An email address** if you are happy for the team to contact you.

You can copy this template and fill it in:

```
Summary:
Area: Login / Checkout / Search / Notifications / Profile / Other
Steps to reproduce:
  1.
  2.
  3.
Expected:
Actual:
Device and OS:
App version:
Frequency: always / sometimes / once
Severity: blocker / major / minor / cosmetic
Contact email (optional):
```

**A good report looks like this:**

> **Summary:** Checkout button does nothing with a saved card.
> **Steps:** 1. Add any item to the basket. 2. Go to Checkout. 3. Choose the saved card. 4. Tap Pay.
> **Expected:** Payment goes through. **Actual:** The button greys out and nothing happens.
> **Device:** Pixel 8, Android 14. **App:** 4.2.1. **Frequency:** always. **Severity:** blocker.

Please do not include passwords or full card numbers. Send me what you have and I will file it.
