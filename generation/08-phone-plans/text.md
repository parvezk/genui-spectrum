## Which phone plan for 12 GB a month and two weeks abroad?

**Recommendation: Plus.** It covers 12 GB with room to spare and costs **$550 a year** including roaming, against $720 for Max. Basic is the most expensive of the three for this usage and does not work abroad at all.

| | Basic | **Plus** | Max |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Monthly price | $25 | $40 | $60 |
| Data included | 5 GB | 20 GB | Unlimited |
| Extra data | $10 per GB | — | — |
| Roaming | Not available | $5 per day | Included |
| **Monthly cost at 12 GB** | **$95** | **$40** | **$60** |
| Roaming for 14 days | Not possible | $70 | $0 |
| **Cost per year** | **$1,140** + no service abroad | **$550** | **$720** |

### How the numbers work out

- **Basic:** you would use 7 GB over the 5 GB allowance. 7 × $10 = $70 in overage, so $25 becomes **$95 a month**, or $1,140 a year. And there is no roaming for the two trips.
- **Plus:** 12 GB fits inside 20 GB, so it stays at $40 a month, $480 a year. Two one-week trips are 14 roaming days × $5 = $70. Total **$550**.
- **Max:** $60 × 12 = **$720**, with data and roaming included.

Plus saves **$170 a year** over Max and **$590** over Basic.

### When the answer would change

- **Choose Max** if you spend more than **48 days a year** abroad (48 × $5 = $240, the price gap between Plus and Max), or if your usage grows past 20 GB a month.
- **Choose Basic** only if you use 5 GB or less and never travel. It becomes more expensive than Plus as soon as you pass 6.5 GB a month.
