## Support chat: quick satisfaction survey

Five short questions, about a minute. Send this to the customer right after the chat ends.

---

**Thanks for chatting with us. How did we do?**

**1. Overall, how satisfied are you with the support you just received?**
1 – Very dissatisfied · 2 – Dissatisfied · 3 – Neutral · 4 – Satisfied · 5 – Very satisfied

**2. Did we resolve your issue?**
Yes · Partly · No

**3. How easy was it to get the help you needed?**
1 – Very difficult · 2 – Difficult · 3 – Neither · 4 – Easy · 5 – Very easy

**4. How likely are you to recommend us to a friend or colleague?**
0 (not at all likely) to 10 (extremely likely)

**5. Anything else you would like to tell us?** *(optional)*
If you scored us 1 or 2 on the first question: what went wrong?
Otherwise: what is one thing we could have done better?

*May we contact you about your feedback?* Yes / No

---

### Why these questions

| # | Measures | Metric |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Satisfaction with this interaction | **CSAT**: share of 4s and 5s |
| 2 | Whether the problem is actually fixed | **Resolution rate** |
| 3 | How much effort it took | **CES** (customer effort score) |
| 4 | Loyalty to the company overall | **NPS**: % scoring 9–10 minus % scoring 0–6 |
| 5 | The reason behind the scores | Free text for follow-up |

### Tips for sending it

- **Send it immediately**, while the chat is still fresh. People are far more likely to answer in the moment than later.
- **Keep question 1 first and answerable in one tap.** Many people stop there, and that single answer is still useful.
- **Follow up on low scores.** A 1 or 2, or a "No" on question 2, should open a ticket for a human to review.
- **Do not add more questions.** Each extra one costs completions.
