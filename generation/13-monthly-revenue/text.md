### What stands out

- **Total for the year: $683k**, an average of about **$56.9k** a month.
- **The second half was much stronger:** $389k in July–December against $294k in January–June, 32% more.
- **Four months fell:** February (−$4k), April (−$4k), July (−$3k) and, most sharply, August (−$9k). The February and August dips were each followed by one of the two biggest jumps of the year: +$13k in March and +$14k in September.
- **Strong finish:** four straight months of growth from September, with every month from September onward above the yearly average.
- **By quarter:** Q1 $131k, Q2 $163k, Q3 $170k, Q4 $219k. Q4 alone brought in 32% of the year's revenue.
