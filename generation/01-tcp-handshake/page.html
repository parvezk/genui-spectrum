The TCP three-way handshake

Step through the three segments that open a connection. Change the starting sequence numbers to see how the acknowledgements follow.

Why three steps and not two?
  • Both directions are confirmed. Each side must learn the other received its sequence number.
  • Stale requests are rejected. A delayed old SYN gets a SYN-ACK that nobody answers, so nothing opens.
  • Numbers are synchronised. All later bytes are counted from x and y, which enables ordering and retransmission.
