## Lisbon vs Prague vs Copenhagen for a May weekend

**Go to Prague.** It is the cheapest by a clear margin at **$515**, and since cost matters most to you, that settles it. **Lisbon** is the runner-up: $160 more buys you the warmest weather of the three. Copenhagen is both the most expensive and the coolest.

| | Lisbon | **Prague** | Copenhagen |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Return flight | $180 | $140 | $210 |
| Hotel, 3 nights | $360 | $270 | $570 |
| Food, 3 days | $135 | $105 | $210 |
| **Total** | **$675** | **$515** | **$990** |
| Average high in May | 22 °C | 19 °C | 16 °C |
| Cost rank | 2nd | 1st | 3rd |
| Weather rank | 1st | 2nd | 3rd |

### How the totals break down

- **Lisbon:** $180 + 3 × $120 + 3 × $45 = **$675**
- **Prague:** $140 + 3 × $90 + 3 × $35 = **$515**
- **Copenhagen:** $210 + 3 × $190 + 3 × $70 = **$990**

Hotels drive the gap. Copenhagen's three nights alone ($570) cost more than the entire Prague trip.

### Weighing cost against weather

- **Prague** wins on cost and is in the middle on weather. 19 °C is comfortable for walking a city all day.
- **Lisbon** costs $160 more (about 31%) for 3 °C more warmth and a better chance of sitting outside in the evening. If weather mattered as much as cost to you, Lisbon would edge ahead.
- **Copenhagen** costs nearly twice as much as Prague and is the coolest, so it loses on both of your criteria.

**Verdict:** Prague, unless the extra warmth is worth $160 to you, in which case Lisbon.
