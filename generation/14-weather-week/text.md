## Lisbon: 7-day forecast

**Best beach day: Tuesday.** It is the warmest day of the week at 26 °C and sunny. Monday (24 °C, sunny) is a good second choice, and Sunday (23 °C, sunny) works if you want to go at the weekend.

### The week at a glance

- **Start of the week is the best of it:** sunny and warming up to Tuesday's peak.
- **Wednesday** is still pleasant at 23 °C, but clouds move in ahead of the rain.
- **Thursday and Friday are wet** and the coolest days, with highs of 19 °C and 18 °C. Plan indoor things: museums, the Oceanário, a long lunch.
- **The weekend recovers:** cloudy but dry on Saturday, then sunny again on Sunday.

### For the beach

- **Go Tuesday.** Bring sunscreen: a clear day in Lisbon means a high UV index even when the air feels mild.
- The Atlantic is cool here, so the water will feel cold whatever the air temperature.
- Evenings drop to 16–17 °C, so pack a light layer if you stay for sunset.
