### What the numbers say

- **Rent dominates:** $1,800 is 54.5% of spending, more than everything else combined ($1,500).
- **Food is the second-biggest area:** groceries and dining out together come to $860, or 26.1%. Dining out alone ($340) is about two-thirds of what you spend on groceries.
- **Essentials vs everything else:** rent, groceries, transport and utilities add up to $2,690 (81.5%). Dining out, subscriptions and "other" make up the flexible $610 (18.5%).
- **Subscriptions are small:** $75 is 2.3%. Cancelling all of them would save less than cutting dining out by a quarter ($85).

### If you want to cut back

The realistic levers are **dining out** ($340) and **other** ($195): together $535 a month. Trimming both by 30% frees up about $160 a month, or $1,926 a year, without touching the essentials. Rent is the only category where a change would move the total a lot, and it is the hardest to change.
